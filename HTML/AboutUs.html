Who We Are

Everything you need.
Right in your hostel.

Let’s be honest. College life doesn’t operate on a 9-to-5 schedule. Your best ideas happen at 2:00 AM, exam preparation requires all-night grinds, and the college mess is never open when you get those sudden hunger pangs.

That’s where we come in. Snackment is your trusted hostel convenience store. We are a 24/7 campus micro-store created by students, specifically for hostel residents. We are your right-down-the-corridor hub for everything you need to survive and thrive in hostel life. No delivery apps, no waiting around, and no stepping foot outside the hostel gates.

Why We Started

We’ve all been there: staring at a tough assignment at 3:00 AM, realizing you ran out of notebooks, or needing a quick energy boost only to find everything outside closed. While hostel life is full of energy and ambition, it has always lacked a reliable, round-the-clock source for quick needs.

We built Snackment to bridge that gap. By setting up a dedicated hub right inside the hostel building, we've made sure that you are never more than a 5-minute walk away from your favorite snacks, drinks, and essential gear.
